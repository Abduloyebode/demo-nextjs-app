# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are operators on small growing teams who need a calm place to track weekly work and extract structured facts from PDFs. They use the product signed-in, often mid-task, scanning for status more than exploring.

*(Inferred from app copy and features; not a live customer interview.)*

## Product Purpose

Northstar Ops helps teams turn priorities into progress with a simple weekly operating rhythm, personal workflows, and AI-assisted document extraction. Success means a user can open the dashboard, see what needs attention, and finish upload or workflow updates without hunting for status.

## Positioning

A small, deployable operating console—not a full work management suite. Auth, org-ready data, background jobs, and document extraction stay in one focused Next.js app.

*(Inferred from Steps 0–5 scope.)*

## Capabilities

- Email/password auth with protected dashboard
- Workflow CRUD with search, filter, and sort
- PDF upload with background AI extraction (pending → processing → completed/failed)
- Document results: title, summary, important dates, obligations, risk level

## Constraints

- Do not invent backend behaviour while redesigning UI
- Keep teal Northstar brand continuity (refine, don’t replace with a new visual world)
- Prefer Operate-mode clarity over marketing spectacle on dashboard surfaces
- Accessibility: keyboard focus, labels, contrast, meaningful empty/error/loading states

## Voice

Calm, direct, operational. Short sentences. No hype, no “boost productivity” filler.

## Brand commitments

- Product name: **Northstar Ops**
- Accent: teal (existing mark uses teal tile “N”)
- Anti-references: purple gradients, glassmorphism, Inter-as-default, cream+terracotta AI templates, dense broadsheet layouts

## Accessibility

Target WCAG AA for text and controls. Minimum touch targets ~40px. Prefer visible focus rings on interactive controls.
